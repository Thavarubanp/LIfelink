title: LifeLink for doctors and hospital staff
---

# Screening queue (doctors)

The **Screening Queue** lists donor screening reports for your hospital. Reports for requests assigned to you are shown first; any active doctor of the same hospital can act as a fallback. Each report shows every answer by section, the flags raised by the rules, and the AI summary and recommendation (Eligible, Temporarily Deferred or Requires Doctor Review). The final decision is always yours:

- **Approve** (optional notes, visible to the donor) reserves one donation slot. Approval is only possible while a slot is free; otherwise the donor waits on standby.
- **Reject** requires a reason, which the donor sees.

Decisions cannot be changed afterwards, and older report versions stay in the history.

# Recording donations

When an approved donor has donated, a doctor or the hospital's staff opens the request and chooses **Record donation**, confirming the tested blood group. This moves the reserved slot to donated units. For requests created by the hospital itself, each donation also becomes a blood packet in the hospital's stock.

# Releasing a reservation

If an approved donor cannot proceed (for example they did not attend or their account was suspended), a doctor or hospital staff member can **Release reservation** with a reason. The slot becomes available again and the donor is told the reason.

# Verifying blood requests (hospital staff)

In **Verify Blood Requests**, verify a pending request and assign one of your doctors, or reject it with a reason.
