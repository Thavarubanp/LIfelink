# What the assistant can do

The LifeLink assistant is available on every page. It can:

- answer questions about blood donation, eligibility, blood groups, storage and emergency procedures, with the sources it used;
- explain how LifeLink works;
- summarise your own requests, donations, screening progress or your hospital's stock, and suggest what to do next;
- run the donor screening interview.

Answers are labelled **Medical guidance** (from published guidelines), **LifeLink guide** (how the platform works) or **Your account** (your own data).

# What the assistant cannot do

- It never approves or rejects donors, verifies donations or makes medical decisions. Doctors and authorised hospital staff make those decisions.
- It cannot see other people's data: other users, hospitals, doctors, complaints, appeals, requests, inventories or notifications. It only sees information you are allowed to see yourself.
- It does not act for you. When it suggests an action, it shows a button that opens the right page.

Messages you send to the assistant are processed by Google Gemini to understand and answer them. Do not share information you do not want processed.

# Profile and eligibility

Your profile shows your blood group, last donation date and next eligible date. You can set your blood group until a recorded donation confirms it, and you can add a donation made outside LifeLink so the 120-day interval is correct.

# Complaints, appeals and suspensions

Use **Complaints** to report a problem with a hospital or user. A suspended account can only use the Governance Portal to see its status and appeal; it cannot take part in donations until reinstated.
