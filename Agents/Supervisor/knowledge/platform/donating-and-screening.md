---
title: Donating through LifeLink
---

# Accepting a request

Open a request in **Available Requests** and choose **Accept & Begin Health Screening**. You need to:

- use a donor account that is active (not suspended or blocked);
- select your blood group (it must be compatible with the request);
- have waited at least 120 days since your last donation;
- have no other donation in progress (one active donation at a time).

# The screening interview

After accepting, the LifeLink screening assistant (the Request Management agent) interviews you step by step in 12 sections: personal information, donation history, health today, basic eligibility, medical history, recent medical events, recent diseases, dental and medication history, travel, a confidential infection-risk section, recent symptoms, and a section for female donors. You can ask what any question or medical term means at any time and the assistant will explain before continuing. You can leave and continue later from **My Acceptances**.

When you finish, the assistant prepares a screening report and sends it to the hospital's assigned doctor. The AI only organises your answers and highlights points for the doctor; it never approves or rejects you.

# Updating your answers

While the doctor has not yet decided, you can choose **Update my answers**. Your previous report is kept (marked "Superseded") and a new version is sent when you finish. After the doctor decides, the report can no longer be changed.

# The doctor's decision

- **Approved:** a donation slot is reserved for you. Visit the hospital to donate; the doctor's notes explain anything you need to know.
- **Not approved:** you will see the doctor's reason in **My Acceptances**. The request stays open for other donors.

# Withdrawing

You can withdraw from **My Acceptances** at any time before your donation is recorded. If a doctor had approved you, your reserved slot is released so another donor can take it. If you cannot attend, please withdraw so the patient is not kept waiting. The hospital may also release a reservation (for example if a donor does not attend), with a reason shown to the donor.

# After donating

The hospital records your donation. Your blood group is confirmed from the test, your next eligible donation date is set 120 days later, and you receive a thank-you notification.
